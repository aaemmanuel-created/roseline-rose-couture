If you change your mind

Returns & cancellations

Last updated 14 November 2026

Each of our pieces is made to order. Here is how cancelling and returns work, in line with the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 and the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

Pieces in our standard sizes (UK 6–18)

If you order a piece in one of our standard sizes online, you may change your mind and cancel within 14 days of the day you receive it, without giving a reason.

  • To cancel, tell us clearly by email at roselinerosecouture@hotmail.com — your name, order number and the piece is enough.
  • Send the piece back within 14 days of telling us. Please return it unworn, as you received it. You pay the cost of returning it, unless it is faulty or not as described.
  • Your refund, including the standard delivery charge you paid, goes back to your original payment method within 14 days of us receiving the piece back (or your proof of posting, if sooner).
  • We may reduce the refund if the piece has been handled more than is needed to try it on, for example worn to an event, altered or marked.

You can also cancel before the piece is sent: email us and we will refund you in full.

Made-to-measure, bespoke and altered pieces

Pieces made to your own measurements or design, ordered as “made to my measurements”, commissioned as bespoke, or altered to your request, are made specially for you. The law’s 14-day right to cancel does not apply to them. Your rights if such a piece is faulty, below, still apply in full. Any deposit and cancellation arrangements for a commission are agreed with you in writing before work starts.

If something is wrong

Every piece must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described. If it is not:

  • within 30 days of receiving it you can reject it for a full refund;
  • after that, you can ask us to repair or replace it, and if that is not possible or does not work, you can have a refund (part or full).

Please email roselinerosecouture@hotmail.com with a photograph and we will arrange the return at our cost.

Questions

If you are not sure which applies to your order, write to us before you buy — we are glad to help.
