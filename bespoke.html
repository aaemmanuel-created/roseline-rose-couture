Bespoke

Nothing is stocked. Every piece is cut for one wearer.

I

Consultation

An unhurried hour. Cloth in hand, the occasion discussed, measurements taken, a sketch made.

II

The toile

A calico first draft, fitted to your body and adjusted on you — before a single length of the real cloth is cut.

III

Final fitting

The finished cloth, the last hand-stitching, the hem set to your shoe. Delivered wrapped in silk and paper.
