Royal Choice

Celebration, purpose, divine elegance.

The inspiration

Born in a season of prayer.

The Royal Choice Collection is inspired by the beauty of celebration, purpose and divine elegance. Each design was created for a special wedding occasion, and tells a story of transformation, confidence and favour.

The vision came during an extended fast with my church, while I was studying the Book of Esther. Reflecting on her journey of faith, prayer, courage and favour, God gave me a personal revelation — and the name was born.

Esther 5 · 1–2

She put on her royal robes.

Esther called God’s people to fast with her. Then she stood before the king — and he looked upon her with favour, and extended the golden sceptre.

For whom

Created to stand out.

Royal Choice is for women who walk with confidence, elegance and purpose — who celebrate life’s most meaningful moments while embracing their own beauty. Every dress is designed to make the wearer feel radiant and unforgettable.
