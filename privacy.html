Privacy notice

Last updated 14 November 2026

This notice explains what personal details Roseline Rose Couture (“we”, “the house”) collects through this website, why, and the rights you have under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Who we are

Roseline Rose Couture is a couture house with an atelier in London. We decide how your details are used, so we are the “controller” of them. You can reach us at roselinerosecouture@hotmail.com.

What we collect

  • When you write to us (the contact form, “Register interest” on a piece, or email): your name, email address and what you tell us — for example your size, event date, occasion and message. Our forms do not store anything on this website: they open your own email app with the message written out, and it reaches us only when you press Send.
  • The house letter: your email address, if you ask to join.
  • When you order: the basket and checkout are run by Shopify. Shopify collects your name, delivery address, contact and payment details to process the order, and passes us what we need to make and deliver it. We never see your full card number.
  • Fittings and bespoke work: your measurements and fitting notes, which you give us in person or by email.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind on this website.

Why we use it, and on what legal basis

PurposeLegal basis

Who else handles your details

We share details only with the services that help us run the house, and only what each needs:

  • Shopify — the basket, checkout and order records. See Shopify’s own privacy policy at checkout.
  • Microsoft (Outlook / Hotmail) — our email, where your messages arrive.
  • GitHub — hosts this website; like any web host it receives your device’s IP address when pages load.
  • Google Fonts — supplies the typefaces on this site; your browser fetches them from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Couriers — your name and delivery address, to deliver your piece.

Some of these companies may handle data outside the UK; where they do, they rely on the safeguards UK law requires (such as UK adequacy regulations or approved contract clauses). We never sell your details or share them for marketing.

Cookies and storage

This website sets no cookies of its own. When you add a piece to your basket, your browser stores one basket reference (rrc-checkout-id) so the basket is still there when you come back. It is strictly necessary for the basket to work, so it does not need consent; clearing your browser’s site data removes it. Shopify’s checkout pages set their own cookies, explained in Shopify’s notices there.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to an order: up to two years after we last hear from you.
  • Orders, measurements and fitting notes: for as long as you are a client, so we can alter or remake your pieces; order and payment records for six years, as tax law requires.
  • The house letter: until you ask to leave.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your details, correct them, delete them, restrict or object to how we use them, or send them to you in a portable form. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Write to roselinerosecouture@hotmail.com; we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your details, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or 0303 123 1113.

Children

Our services are for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you.

Changes

If we change this notice we will update the date at the top of this page.
